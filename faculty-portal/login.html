Oral exams
reimagined

AI Oral Exam brings the conversation of an oral exam online, without scheduling every student one by one. Faculty upload their course material, review the questions the AI writes from it, and publish the exam. Students take it on their own schedule, answering out loud to an AI examiner that asks follow-up questions the way a professor would.

  1. FacultyUpload course material
  2. FacultyReview and publish questions
  3. StudentAnswer the AI examiner out loud
  4. FacultyReview transcripts and results
